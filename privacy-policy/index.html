Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

1. Introduction

Pedro Thiago Data Analyst ("we," "us," or "our") operates data analytics and business intelligence consulting services. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use our services, applications, and integrations, including any tools or software that connect to third-party advertising and business platforms via their official APIs.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect and process the following types of information:

  • Account & Contact Information: Name, email address, company name, and other contact details you provide when engaging our services.
  • Advertising Platform Data: Campaign performance data, ad metrics, and reporting data retrieved from authorized advertising accounts on platforms such as Meta Ads, Google Ads, TikTok Ads, and LinkedIn Ads, accessed through their official APIs with your explicit authorization.
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, device information, and usage data collected automatically when you interact with our applications or websites.
  • Authorization Tokens: OAuth access tokens and refresh tokens used to maintain authorized connections to third-party platforms on your behalf.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To provide, maintain, and improve our BI consulting and data analytics services.
  • To retrieve and consolidate advertising performance data from authorized platforms for reporting and dashboard purposes.
  • To communicate with you regarding our services, updates, and support.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms of service.

We do not use your data to build user profiles, serve advertisements, or for any purpose beyond the scope of our contracted services.

4. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or advertising data to any third parties. We may share data only in the following circumstances:

  • With your consent: When you explicitly authorize us to share data with specific parties.
  • Service providers: With trusted infrastructure providers (such as cloud hosting services) that process data on our behalf under strict confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal requirements: When required by law, regulation, or legal process.

5. Third-Party Platform Data

When you authorize our applications to access your advertising accounts on platforms such as Meta, Google, TikTok, or LinkedIn, we access only the data scopes you have explicitly authorized. We comply with each platform's developer terms of service, data use policies, and API usage guidelines. You may revoke our access to your accounts at any time through the respective platform's settings.

6. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including encryption of data in transit (TLS/SSL), secure storage of authentication tokens, access controls, and regular security reviews. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

We retain your data only for as long as necessary to provide our services or as required by law. Authorization tokens are stored securely and refreshed as needed. When our services are terminated, we will delete your data within 30 days unless retention is required by applicable law.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal data.
  • Withdraw consent for data processing at any time.
  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format.
  • Object to or restrict certain types of data processing.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at the email address provided in the Contact section.

9. Cookies & Tracking

Our website may use essential cookies to ensure basic functionality. We do not use tracking cookies, third-party analytics, or advertising pixels on our website.

10. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of our services after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
